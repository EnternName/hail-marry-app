# Hail Mary 🚀

An **Astrophage growth simulator** inspired by *Project Hail Mary* by Andy Weir.

- Sliders control how fast the Astrophage swarm doubles and how big it starts.
- The ↺ button resets the time, the sliders and the camera to where they started.
- A 3D view shows the swarm spreading around the Sun, the infrared "Petrova line" streaming toward Venus, and Earth icing over as the Sun dims. Drag to rotate. The background is a turning starfield with the Milky Way, nebulae and the odd shooting star.
- Earth's cooling uses the Stefan–Boltzmann law: temperature follows the fourth root of the sunlight a planet receives.

## Talk to Rocky 🎵

The second tab is a language game. Rocky speaks Eridian, a language of musical chords, and you learn it:

- **Talk:** 10-question rounds. Either Rocky says a word and you pick its meaning, or you're given a word and pick the right sound to say it back. Correct answers earn XP and build streaks.
- **Levels:** 24 words across 6 levels. Get 8 out of 10 in a round to unlock the next level.
- **Words:** hear any unlocked word and see its note pattern. Turn the note patterns off in the Talk tab to play by ear for double XP.
- Progress is saved in your browser. Turn the sound up (on iPhone, also flip the silent switch off).

Built with [Flutter](https://flutter.dev). Every push to `main` builds the web version and publishes it with GitHub Pages.

**Play it:** https://enternname.github.io/hail-marry-app/ — on iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
